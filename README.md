
# SmartSched  
## Student Life Scheduler Built for Balance

## Overview

This is a student-focused web application that helps you schedule your day in a way that reflects how life actually works by learning from your experiences.

This is not just any other productivity tool, but SmartSched isn't about packing more in. It is about building a schedule that accounts for all of you: your classes, study blocks, gym sessions, job, commute, and the time you spend with people who matter. The goal is a week that's full without being overwhelming, and a routine you can actually sustain across an entire semester.

Our goal here isn't to help you do more; it is to help you not burn out doing what already matters.

## The Problem

College students juggle more than coursework. Between commuting, working jobs, staying active, and maintaining friendships, finding time to study is genuinely hard. Existing tools either:

- Treat the calendar as a productivity grid (eg: Google Calendar, Notion)
- Focus narrowly on academics (class schedulers, syllabus trackers, eg: Canvas)
- Optimize for output rather than wellbeing

None of them mold the schedule to your needs, and you end up doing that on your own. 

Students who try to fit everything in without a thoughtful system often end up skipping meals, ghosting friends, or cramming at 2am — not because they're lazy, but because seeing the full picture without any support is really hard.

## Our Approach

- Consistent, moderate effort over time beats sprint-and-crash cycles.
- Your shift at work, your gym session, and your coffee with a friend are not obstacles to your schedule; they *are* your schedule.
- If your plan leaves no room for rest, your plan is broken.
- Success isn't finishing your to-do list. It's showing up for yourself and others over the long run.


## Core Features (Planned)

- Input all your commitments — classes, work shifts, gym sessions, social plans, and commute times and get a unified daily/weekly view that reflects your real life.
- Account for travel time between locations (campus, gym, workplace, home) so your schedule doesn't assume you can teleport.
- Based on your energy patterns, free windows, and upcoming deadlines, the app suggests realistic study blocks — without overloading any single day.
- If your week is looking overloaded(eg: back-to-back obligations, no breaks, insufficient sleep windows), the app flags it and suggests adjustments rather than letting you run yourself into the ground.
- Gym, social time, and personal downtime are treated as first-class schedule items, not sacrificial blocks that get deleted when things get busy.

What we don't want to become: A rigid productivity optimizer, class registration tool. habit tracker with streaks and gamification pressure, Google Calendar clone

## Target Users

College students — especially those who:
- Commute to campus
- Hold part-time or full-time jobs alongside their studies
- Are trying to maintain a social life and physical health
- Have struggled with burnout, overwhelm, or inconsistent routines
